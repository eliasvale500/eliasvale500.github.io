Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

This is the website of The Last Witness, a historical horror series by Elias Vale. This page explains what information the site handles and what you can do about it.

If you only visit the site

The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising trackers, and does not ask for any personal information. Your language choice is saved in your own browser only, so the page opens in the same language next time. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical data such as your IP address when it delivers the page; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Links to YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and Wikipedia take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

If you subscribe to episode alerts

If you enter your email address in the sign-up form and tick the consent box, we collect:

  • your email address;
  • a record of your consent: the date and time you subscribed and confirmed, and the form you used.

We use your email address for one purpose: to send you a short email when a new episode of The Last Witness is released, and occasional news about the series. We do not sell, rent or share your address with anyone else.

After you subscribe, you receive an email asking you to confirm. You are only added to the list once you click that link.

Who stores your email

The list is kept by our email provider, Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France), which sends the emails on our behalf and stores the data under its own security and privacy commitments. Brevo may count whether an email was opened or a link was clicked so we can see if the alerts are useful.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. We keep the record of your consent for as long as the law requires us to be able to show it.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe: every email has an unsubscribe link. It works immediately.
  • See, correct or delete your data: email eliasvale.caa@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days.
  • Complaints: if you are in Canada, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada. In the EU or UK, you can contact your local data protection authority.

Age

The series is not made for children, and the sign-up form is not meant for anyone under 16.
