Who he is
Elias Vale, born in the year 500. He does not grow old. He is a fictional character. Episode 1
The rule
His family has kept one rule for nine generations: when it calls, you do not answer. Episode 1
The knock
In every catastrophe, something knocks at the door in the voice of someone the listener has lost. Whoever answers is marked with a circle on the palm. Episode 10
The ledger
Elias keeps a ledger of every disaster he has survived, with the year and the place. Episode 9
Today
Istanbul, 2025. Elias tells what he saw to Kemal, a carpet seller, and his grandson Emre. Episode 10
